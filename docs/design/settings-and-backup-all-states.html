C · Segment — corner anatomy
light
Corner logic by position: a single-row group is fully rounded (16px); a first row curves the top and softens the meeting edge to 2px; middle rows are 2px all around; the last row softens the top and curves the bottom. Segments sit on a 2px seam of scaffold — no divider lines. Light mode adds the two-layer ambient shadow (0/1/2 @6%, 0/4/12 @8%) and the 7% hairline; dark mode lifts the fill to #181818 and drops the shadow.

D · Automatic backups — the four panel states
light
The new panel at the top of Backup and restore. From top: saved — toggle on, “Last saved Today 14:32 · 6 classes.” · saving — “Saving a fresh backup…” right after a change · failed — subtitle turns red and Klasify retries on the next change · paused — toggle off; the copy asks the user back. The backup file itself lives in the app support folder and rides with Android’s own Google device backup — no account, no network permission.

Backup and restore
Protect your week
Klasify saves a fresh backup after every change — no button to press. The copy stays on this device and rides with your phone’s Google backup when device backup is on. A file export is here for moving to a new phone.
