Klasify

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026  ·  Applies to the Klasify Android and iOS apps

In short: Klasify works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no cloud, no analytics, and no advertising. Your schedule never leaves your phone. The only feature that can ever transmit anything is optional crash reporting, which exists only in builds where the developer explicitly enabled it, and which sends technical crash diagnostics — never your schedule, class names, professors, or notes.

1. Who we are

Klasify is an offline-first weekly class timetable app for students, developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). It is distributed on Google Play and, where applicable, the Apple App Store.

2. Data stored on your device

All app data is created and stored locally in the app's private database on your device:

  • Schedule data — subjects, rooms, professors, days, and times you enter or import.
  • Class status marks — done / skipping / cancelled marks and their time ranges.
  • App settings — reminders enabled, onboarding completed, theme preferences.
  • Imported images and PDFs — processed on-device by built-in OCR. They are not uploaded anywhere, and no model download is required.

This data is never sent to us or to any server. Android's system backup is configured to exclude the schedule database and app preferences; the in-app JSON backup export exists so you can move your schedule yourself. Exported backup files are ordinary files you control — wherever you save or send them is your choice, outside this app.

3. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any of the following, in any build of the app:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account details — the app has no login.
  • Schedule content, class names, rooms, professor names, or notes.
  • Location data, contacts, photos, or files other than those you explicitly pick for import.
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data of any kind.
  • Usage analytics, screen recording, or behavioral profiling.

4. Optional crash reporting (the only network feature)

Distribution builds of Klasify can include the Sentry crash-reporting SDK so that crashes on real devices can be diagnosed and fixed. It is enabled only when the developer compiles a Sentry configuration into that specific build, and it remains completely dormant otherwise — builds without it open no network connections at all.

When active, a crash report may contain:

  • Device model and manufacturer, operating system version, and app version.
  • The error's stack trace, timestamp, and technical breadcrumbs (app lifecycle and navigation events).

Crash reports are configured to exclude personal identifiers, screenshots, and any schedule content. Reports captured while offline are stored temporarily on the device and sent when a connection is available. Reports are retained on Sentry's servers per Sentry's retention policy (approximately 90 days) and are visible only to the developer. Sentry's own privacy policy applies to that service: sentry.io/privacy.

Because the SDK is present in these builds, the Android INTERNET permission appears in the app manifest. The permission itself grants no access to your data; it only permits the network connection described above.

6. Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its app data) permanently deletes your schedule and settings — export a JSON backup first if you want to keep them. Crash reports already sent to Sentry cannot be recalled, but they contain no identifiers linking them to you, and they expire automatically under Sentry's retention policy.

7. Children and students

Klasify is a general-audience study tool and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information in any build, no age gate or parental consent mechanism is needed.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: contact the developer using the support email shown under Developer contact on Klasify's store listing (Google Play or the App Store). That mailbox reaches the same person who operates this app and this page.

Klasify is offline-first software. This page is the complete privacy statement for the app and is intended to be published at a public URL referenced by the store listings.
