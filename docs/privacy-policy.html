Klasify

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026  ·  Applies to the Klasify Android and iOS apps

In short: Klasify works on your device first. Signed out, it has no account, no cloud, and no advertising, and your schedule never leaves your phone. You can optionally sign in to a self-hosted Klasify server to sync your schedule, campus events, and office availability across devices. When you are signed in, the account details and schedule data described below are sent to that server. There is no analytics service and no advertising network.

1. Who we are

Klasify is an offline-first weekly class timetable app for students, developed by an independent developer ("we", "us", "the operator"). It is distributed on Google Play and, where applicable, the Apple App Store. The optional sync backend is self-hosted by the operator; it is not a third-party cloud service.

2. Data stored on your device

All app data is created and stored locally in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Schedule data — subjects, rooms, professors, days, and times you enter or import.
  • Class status marks — done / skipping / cancelled marks and their time ranges.
  • Events — personal events you create, plus campus and department events you have synced.
  • App settings — reminders enabled, onboarding completed, theme preferences, and the active account scope.
  • Imported images and PDFs — processed on-device by built-in OCR. They are not uploaded anywhere, and no model download is required.

The raw SQLite database and app preferences are excluded from Android's system backup. The app's own JSON backup file (auto_backups/), which contains your schedule, is included in Android's device cloud backup and device transfer when you have those Android features enabled. That copy is controlled by your Android/Google account settings, not by Klasify. You can also export a JSON backup yourself; wherever you save or send that file is your choice and outside this app.

3. Optional accounts and sync

Signing in is optional. You can use the whole timetable, reminders, and backup features without an account. When you sign in, sync turns on automatically. The app talks only to the Klasify server configured for that build; release builds require an HTTPS connection.

How you can sign in

  • Google Sign-In — Google verifies your identity and shares your Google subject identifier, email address, and display name with Klasify. Google's handling of your Google account is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Email and password — the server stores your email, display name, department, and a bcrypt password hash. The password itself is never stored in readable form. Password-reset codes are sent to your email address.

What the app sends to the server

  • Your account details: display name, email address, department, and (for Google accounts) the Google subject identifier.
  • Your schedule: classes (subject, room, professor, weekday, start and end time), class status marks, and events (title, venue, date, time, category, reminder lead time, and department for broadcasts).
  • If you are an office administrator, the campus office availability you edit. Office availability is downloaded by signed-in users so it can be shown offline.
  • Technical sync records: a device identifier, per-table sync timestamps, and the mapping between local row ids and server row ids.

Why — to authenticate you, keep your schedule consistent across your devices, deliver campus and department events, and show office availability. We do not sell this data, share it for advertising, or use it for tracking. The server stores it for as long as your account exists, as described in section 7.

4. Data we do NOT collect

Klasify never collects, transmits, or sells:

  • Phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identifiers.
  • Location data, contacts, or photos and files other than those you explicitly pick for import.
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data of any kind.
  • Usage analytics, screen recording, or behavioral profiling.
  • Schedule content from users who are signed out.

Your email address and display name are collected only if you choose to create an account.

5. Optional crash reporting

Distribution builds of Klasify can include the Sentry crash-reporting SDK so that crashes on real devices can be diagnosed and fixed. It is enabled only when the developer compiles a Sentry configuration into that specific build, and it remains completely dormant otherwise. This is independent of account sync.

When active, a crash report may contain:

  • Device model and manufacturer, operating system version, and app version.
  • The error's stack trace, timestamp, and technical breadcrumbs (app lifecycle and navigation events).

Crash reports are configured to exclude personal identifiers, screenshots, and any schedule content. Reports captured while offline are stored temporarily on the device and sent when a connection is available. Reports are retained on Sentry's servers per Sentry's retention policy (approximately 90 days) and are visible only to the developer. Sentry's own privacy policy applies to that service: sentry.io/privacy.

7. Deleting your data

Local data: uninstalling the app or clearing its app data permanently deletes the schedule and settings on that phone. The app's JSON backup file is deleted with the app; export it first if you want to keep it. A copy included in Android's device cloud backup follows your Android/Google backup settings.

Account data: use Delete account in the app's Personal details screen. This marks your account and its synced schedule as deleted on the server, ends your sessions, and removes the server's device and sync records for that account. The local schedule stays on the phone in the signed-out store until you remove it. Server-side soft-deleted rows are not returned to any client; contact support if you need help erasing them.

Crash reports already sent to Sentry cannot be recalled, but they contain no identifiers linking them to you, and they expire automatically under Sentry's retention policy.

8. Children and students

Klasify is a general-audience study tool and is not directed at children under 13. Account creation requires an email address, and we do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us using the support contact below so it can be removed.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page with a new effective date. The in-app Privacy Policy screen is updated alongside this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: contact the developer using the support email shown under Developer contact on Klasify's store listing (Google Play or the App Store). That mailbox reaches the same person who operates this app and this page.
